# Project Aurora Brief

Project Aurora connects smart-city sensor data with a simulated urban market
model. The pilot compares traffic, energy and retail demand signals across
Hamburg, Munich, Cologne and Berlin.

The steering goal is to reduce evening congestion by 11 percent while keeping
retail delivery delays below 6 minutes. The project team expects the first
public dashboard in Q4 2026.

Key partners:

- Northstar Mobility provides anonymized traffic counters.
- Stadtwerke Nord provides energy load windows for Hamburg and Cologne.
- MarketPulse Labs maintains the demand simulation model.
- Civic Data Trust reviews privacy and data minimization.

Priority topics for live RAG:

- Hamburg has the highest privacy review urgency because two sensor corridors
  cross residential school zones.
- Munich has the largest projected revenue uplift for local merchants.
- Cologne has the highest grid flexibility benefit.
- Berlin has the broadest stakeholder group and the highest coordination risk.
